my first blog

august 17, 2026

This personal website has been something i wanted to make for YEARS and finally on a random day in august i decided to just do it. I started with a pinterest board of all the different portfolios and websites i found interesting. After finding my theme i went to procreate to sketch it all out to figure out exactly how i wanted the home page to look like. When i created the home page i opened up figma to continue with the other pages. I wanted it to be minimal so i dont face problems later. I want it to be simple and flexible so i can change it whenever i please. After creating the design i bought my domain on porkbun and then connected to a repo on github. Here began the actual coding on vscode and sending back to github for it be hosted on there.

Ok enough of that talk. If you have questions about the logistics and creating this website, just contact me. This will be so fun to create and share more with the world. I currently have 5 pages on my website. The home is just a static page with images i like and my name in the middle. Next the blog page so i can yap forever, about me to get to know me, gallery so i can post photos of what im doing and where i am going, and finally the tv & film page to share reviews of any type of media i consume. Ttyl.
